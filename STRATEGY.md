# SABI AI: Strategic Blueprint
## DSN X BCT LLM Agent Challenge 2026 (Winner's Circle)

### SECTION 1: RESEARCH & OPPORTUNITY ANALYSIS
**The Problem: The "Trust & Discovery" Friction in Nigerian Commerce.**
Nigeria is an informal, trust-based economy. 80% of commerce happens via "Social Commerce" (Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok). However, these platforms lack a structured behavioral intelligence layer.
*   **The Data Gap**: Reviews on global platforms (Amazon/Yelp) are "Western-coded." They don't capture the nuance of "Naija" service quality, price haggling, and "Vibe" compatibility.
*   **The Opportunity**: Build an AI system that translates global datasets (Yelp/Amazon) into a Nigerian Behavioral Engine.
*   **Why Judges Love It**: It bridges the gap between massive global datasets and local economic reality.

### SECTION 4: TASK A - USER MODELING (BEHAVIORAL FIDELITY)
**The Strategy: "The Persona Mirror"**
To win Task A, we don't just predict ratings; we simulate the *writer*.
1.  **Linguistic Layer**: We use "Naija-Linguist Agent" to inject local semantics (Pidgin, slang, tone) into review generation.
2.  **ROUGE/BERTScore Optimization**: 
    - Construct a "Behavioral Embedding" of the user from their Amazon/Yelp history.
    - Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) of similar "tonal" reviews to guide the LLM.
3.  **RMSE Optimization**:
    - Use a dedicated "Rating Oracle Agent" that reconciles the text sentiment with the numerical rating using cross-domain transfer learning.

---

### SECTION 5: TASK B - RECOMMENDATION (AGENTIC PRECISION)
**The Strategy: "Relational Ranking"**
1.  **Context-Aware Ranking**: Standard algorithms miss "contextual suitability." Our "Context Agent" analyzes time of day, current user mood, and "Nigerian factor" (e.g., traffic patterns, power supply constraints) before ranking.
2.  **NDCG@10 Optimization**:
    - **Step 1: Multi-Domain Retrieval**: Pull candidates from Amazon (Product), Yelp (Service), and Goodreads (Style).
    - **Step 2: Behavioral Filtering**: Filter candidates through the user's "Psychological Profile."
    - **Step 3: Multi-Turn Reasoning**: The agent explains *why* it recommended X over Y based on the user's past disappointments.

---

### SECTION 6: AGENTIC WORKFLOW DESIGN
We deploy a **"Council of Experts"**:
*   **Psychology Agent**: Builds the deeper "Hidden Preference" profile.
*   **Localization Agent**: Maps global reviews to local Nigerian equivalents (e.g., "Fine dining in NYC" -> "Luxury lounge in VI").
*   **Critique Agent**: Self-reflects on generated reviews to ensure they don't sound "robotic."

---

### SECTION 7: RESEARCH NOVELTY (THE "X-FACTOR")
1.  **B-CoT (Behavioral Chain of Thought)**: Forcing the model to reason through "What would a Lagosian think about this price point?" before generating a rating.
2.  **Cross-Market Transfer (CMT)**: Using Amazon reviews to predict a user's taste in Nigerian SME services.
